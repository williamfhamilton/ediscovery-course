Electronic Discovery — International Cross-Border Discovery
Same email, two sovereigns
Palmetto Ridge Logistics, Inc. & Palmetto Ridge Europe SAS — Cross-Border Scenario
The idea to carry through this exercise

Rights are not a property of the person or of the document. They are created and protected by the sovereign where the person and the data sit — and that varies from sovereign to sovereign.

A United States lawyer is used to a single, fairly uniform body of procedural law. Across a border, the same email can carry a duty to produce in one country and a criminal exposure for producing it in another, at the same moment.

Background

The company: Palmetto Ridge Logistics, Inc., the Tampa freight company from Module 1, has acquired a small French logistics firm, Palmetto Ridge Europe SAS, based in Lyon. Darnell Washington’s discrimination suit is pending in the Middle District of Florida. Plaintiff has served a Rule 34 request for all communications about promotion decisions across the company’s operations, including Lyon.

The custodian: Camille Laurent, a Lyon-based operations supervisor. In February she was copied on a thread with Operations Manager Kyle Reeves about how the company picks “culture fit” candidates for lead roles. The thread sits on a mail server in Lyon. She also discussed it with Reeves on a personal messaging app on her own phone.
